Columns

  • ↓ fills strictly from 1 down to Y.
  • ↕ is the free column.
  • ↑ fills strictly from Y up to 1.
  • Announcement — choose the locked declaration after the first roll; after roll 3 the announced result is written automatically.
  • Hand — may be scored only after the first roll.

Rows

  • 1–6 record matching dice only: count × face value.
  • Σ below 1–6 is automatic; a base subtotal of 60+ receives the +30 upper-section bonus.
  • + records the highest five-dice sum you choose to keep; − records the lowest.
  • The difference row is automatic: max(0, (MAX − MIN) × row 1).
  • S = Straight, F = Full House, P = Four of a Kind, Y = Yamb.
  • The final Σ rows and grand total are calculated by the runtime; they are never manually written.

Scoring

  • 1–6: matching dice count × face value.
  • Straight: 66 points on roll 1, 56 on roll 2, 46 on roll 3.
  • Full House: dice value + 30; Four of a Kind: four matching dice + 40; Yamb: five matching dice + 50.
  • + and − score the sum of all five dice at the moment of writing.
  • Bonuses, section subtotals and the final total are calculated automatically.

Dice

  • Before the first roll, the five dice display Y, A, the crest, M and B in the active blue or red theme, with the action die on the right.
  • Dice inherit the active player’s Royal Navy blue or deep red theme, with metallic gold/silver detailing and a clear HELD state.
  • Tap a die to hold or release it; tap the action die to start the next roll.
  • Each roll uses rotation, bounce and layered impact audio against the wooden tray.

Match

  • The top display always shows the current state of both players.
  • The semaphore tracks games won in the series. The first player or team to five wins takes the match.
  • The interface theme follows the player or team whose turn is active.
  • Single · Solo is Player 1 completing one score sheet; Single · Duel plays against Harbour AI; Hotseat · Duel is 1 vs 1 on one device; Hotseat · Team is 2 vs 2 with two teammate sessions per team turn.
  • Team · TM: each team uses one shared score sheet. Teammate 1 proposes one legal write after a normal up-to-three-roll turn; teammate 2 receives the same fresh chance against the unchanged paper. The team then commits exactly one proposal. If both proposals target the same cell, the higher value is committed automatically.
  • Every completed game is archived in Match History with immutable final paper snapshots.

Online · Live P2P

  • Online supports DUEL, TEAM and CREW: DUEL is 1 vs 1; TEAM is 2 vs 2 with two teammate sessions per team device; CREW assigns A1, A2, B1 and B2 to four independent devices.
  • Fast join: the host receives a QR code and invite link as soon as the room opens. Scanning the QR opens this app with the room and format preloaded. Manual Room ID entry remains supported.
  • No cloud game database and no turn-transfer codes are used. PeerJS uses its public PeerServer only to broker the WebRTC connection; after the data channel opens, game state travels directly browser-to-browser.
  • Only the browser that owns the current turn may roll, hold dice, announce or confirm a score. The other browser is locked automatically.
  • Incoming state is applied only when its revision is newer than the local revision; the local seat assignment is never overwritten.
  • If the direct data connection closes, gameplay locks until the connection is restored. Some restrictive networks can require a TURN relay.
